Visual Graph Programming: mapping modules, assets, and storyboards

Date: 2026-08-01
Categories: Product, Visualization

Hyperion Corporation is building Visual Graph Programming (VGP)—tools that help developers, artists, and designers understand how code modules, assets, and storyboards relate, and create new modules from that shared model.

Why graphs

Large interactive projects bury structure across repositories, asset libraries, and narrative boards. A graph and metrics surface makes those relationships inspectable in 2D/3D without forcing every role into the same IDE.

Who it is for

  • Programmers navigating module boundaries and dependencies
  • Artists and designers connecting assets to systems and boards
  • Teams working on games, simulations, and related computer systems

Status

VGP is in progress. This organization site is the public face of Hyperion on GitHub Pages; product repositories live under the Hyperion-Corporation organization.
