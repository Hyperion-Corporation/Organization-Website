Optimizing creative and engineering workflows

Date: 2026-07-15
Categories: Engineering, Workflows

Hyperion’s mission is to create software that optimizes the human work of developers, artists, and designers. That means reducing the cost of understanding structure, measuring what matters, and bridging technical and creative roles.

Friction we care about

  • Reconstructing how modules and assets connect when ownership is split
  • Storyboards and design docs that drift from implementation
  • Metrics that report volume without revealing architecture

Approach

Prefer shared visual models (graphs, metrics, progressive detail) so multidisciplinary teams can reason together about games, simulations, and other complex interactive systems.
